### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/engine` — a `ref` on an `element` predicate is answered with `query`, not with advice to move it beside `until`.** `misplacedCallFields` reported every `ref` as a CALL argument one level too deep. That is right for the flat kinds, and misdirection for `element`, whose locator nests under `query`: moving a `ref` up on `act_and_wait` retargets the action, and `reticle_assert` has no `until` at all — so the advice cost the caller its target and the predicate still did not parse. An `element` predicate carrying a `ref` now says element predicates take `query` (role/name/testid/text), not `ref`, beside the example the rejection already carried (#445). The move-beside-`until` clause stays for the kinds where it is right. Fixes [#1374](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1374).
